Our Story

Soyab Akhtar Electronics started as a small counter selling spare parts and quick repairs. Word travelled fast because the advice was always honest — if a cheaper option genuinely worked better, that's what we recommended.

That same principle runs the store today. We've grown into a full electronics destination, but the goal hasn't changed: help people buy the right device once, rather than the wrong one twice.

Every product on our floor is tested before it's shelved, and every customer leaves knowing exactly how to use what they bought.
